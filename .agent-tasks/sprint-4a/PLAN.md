# Sprint 4A: Statistical Distribution and Error Modeling of Reconstructed Delta

## 1. System Architecture & Statistical Formulation

Sprint 4A establishes empirical error modeling, tail behavior, and operational false-alarm vs. detection tradeoff bounds for reconstructed sensor attack trajectories $\hat{\boldsymbol{\delta}}$ generated by the Phase 3 champion estimators:
- Offline Step-MAP: `StepMAP_TCN_Declared`, `StepMAP_TCN_Undeclared`
- Online Causal Step-MAP: `CausalStepMAP_TCN_Declared`, `CausalStepMAP_TCN_Undeclared`
- Comparative Baselines: `PersistenceEstimator`, `CompanionRegressionEstimator`

### 1.1 Empirical Error Decomposition
For each scenario evaluation $i \in \{1, \dots, 1080\}$, we analyze:
1. Scenario-level signed amplitude reconstruction error:
   $$e_{A, i} = \hat{A}_i - A_{\text{true}, i} = \text{signed\_amplitude\_error\_train\_std}_i \times \sigma_{\text{train}}$$
2. Pointwise trajectory error across support ($t \in [\text{onset}, \text{offset})$) and non-support ($t \notin [\text{onset}, \text{offset})$):
   $$e_{t, i} = \hat{\delta}_{t, i} - \delta_{\text{true}, t, i}$$

### 1.2 Parametric Distribution Modeling
We fit three candidate continuous parametric distributions to the empirical error distribution $p(e_A)$ and evaluate goodness-of-fit:
1. **Gaussian Distribution**: $\mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$ (baseline symmetry and finite variance assumption).
2. **Student-$t$ Distribution**: $t(\nu, \mu, \sigma)$ with degrees of freedom $\nu > 0$ to capture heavy-tail phenomena and kurtosis.
3. **Generalized Error Distribution (GED / Subbotin)**: $\text{GED}(\mu, \sigma, \beta)$ with shape parameter $\beta > 0$ ($\beta=1$ Laplace, $\beta=2$ Gaussian, $\beta < 2$ heavy/fat-tailed).
4. **Goodness-of-Fit Ledger**: Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) statistic and $p$-value, Akaike Information Criterion (AIC), Bayesian Information Criterion (BIC), and log-likelihood.

### 1.3 Null Clean False-Alarm Distribution $p(\hat{\delta} \mid \delta = 0)$
Under the null hypothesis of authentic telemetry ($H_0: \boldsymbol{\delta} = \mathbf{0}$):
- Reconstructed $\hat{\boldsymbol{\delta}}$ is governed by the ADMM shrinkage operators against surrogate residuals $\epsilon_t \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_\epsilon^2)$ with $\sigma_\epsilon = 0.0418\,\text{m}$.
- We quantify the empirical sparsity probability $P(\hat{\delta}_t = 0 \mid \delta=0)$ and model the conditional tail distribution $p(|\hat{\delta}_t| \mid |\hat{\delta}_t| > 0, \delta=0)$.

### 1.4 Operational Detection Tradeoff Curves ($\tau_{\text{alarm}}$)
For an alarm threshold $\tau > 0$, an attack is declared at time $t$ if $|\hat{\delta}_t| \ge \tau$:
- False Positive Rate (FPR): $\alpha(\tau) = P(|\hat{\delta}_t| \ge \tau \mid H_0)$
- Detection Power / Recall (TPR): $\beta(\tau, A) = P(|\hat{\delta}_t| \ge \tau \mid H_1(A))$
- Determine the minimum operational threshold $\tau^*$ guaranteeing $\text{FAR} \le 1.0\%$ and $\text{FAR} \le 0.1\%$.
